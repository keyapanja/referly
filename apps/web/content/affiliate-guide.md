# Your partner guide

Everything you need to promote, track what you earn and get paid. If something here does not match what you see, the business running the program may have switched that feature off.

## 1. How you get paid, in one page

You share a link or a coupon code. When someone buys through it, the sale is attributed to you and a commission is created. The commission waits for a holding period (so refunds can settle), then becomes payable, and the business pays it in a batch. Every step is visible to you, and nothing about your balance ever changes silently: each movement is a line in your statement.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Offers you may promote, and your rate | Offers |
| Your links and coupon codes | Links & Codes |
| Banners, files and ready-made copy | Assets |
| Sales attributed to you | Conversions |
| Signups and enquiries you sent | Leads |
| What you have earned and what is owed | Earnings |
| How and when you get paid | Payouts |

## 2. Getting started

1. **Accept the terms.** You accept them when you apply or accept an invitation. If the business changes them, you are asked to accept the new version before you carry on.
2. **Check your details** under Profile: your name, email and payout country. Set a password you keep safe.
3. **Create your first link** (below) and put it somewhere real. Nothing is tracked until a link of yours is used.

## 3. Links and coupon codes

**Create a link** (Links & Codes → New link): pick the offer and, optionally, a label. The label is only for you: make one link per placement (`instagram-bio`, `newsletter-march`, `review-post`) and you can tell later which one worked.

Your link looks like `https://.../r/ab12cd34ef`. When someone clicks it:

- the click is recorded against you,
- a cookie is set in that visitor's browser for the program's attribution window (30 days in most programs; the exact number is on the Offers page),
- and they land on the business's page.

If they buy inside that window, the sale is yours, subject to the program's rules.

**Coupon codes**: some programs give you a code instead of, or as well as, a link. A sale that uses your code is attributed to you even with no click at all, which is what makes codes work well for podcasts, video and print. Codes appear under Links & Codes when the business creates one for you.

**What breaks tracking**, worth knowing before you blame the platform:

- Shortening or rewriting your link so the `ref` parameter is lost.
- Sending people to the business's home page rather than through your link.
- A buyer who clears cookies, switches device, or takes longer than the attribution window.
- Embedding the link in an image, iframe or script rather than a real link: those are refused as click stuffing.

If you think a sale of yours went unattributed, raise it as a dispute (section 8) with the order reference; the business can correct attribution.

## 4. Assets and approved copy

**Assets** holds the banners, files and text the business has approved. Text assets are personalised for you: where the copy says `{{link}}`, you see your own tracking link, and the same for your coupon code, your name, the offer and your commission. So you can copy a post and publish it without editing anything.

Use the copy as written unless the business says otherwise. Making claims they have not approved is the fastest way to lose a partnership, and in most programs it breaks the terms you accepted.

## 5. Campaigns

A campaign is a time-boxed promotion: often a better rate, sometimes a bonus for hitting a number of sales or an amount of revenue. Campaigns you have been invited to appear under Campaigns. **Join** the campaign to unlock its rate and its creative; the improved rate applies to sales made while the campaign is live, and the bonus, if there is one, is paid once you cross the threshold.

## 6. Conversions and leads

**Conversions** lists sales attributed to you: when, which offer, the amount the commission was calculated on, and the state of that commission. **Leads** lists signups, bookings or enquiries you sent, if the program pays per lead. A lead is worth nothing until the business qualifies it; you see the status and what it pays, never the person's contact details.

Statuses you will see:

- **Pending**: recorded, inside the holding period.
- **Approved / payable**: cleared, waiting for the next payout.
- **Paid**: sent to you.
- **Reversed**: the customer refunded or cancelled, so the commission was taken back per the program's refund policy.
- **Duplicate** (leads): the same person had already been sent inside the program's dedupe window, so it earns nothing.

## 7. Earnings and payouts

**Earnings** is your ledger: every credit, reversal, adjustment and payout, oldest to newest, with a printable statement for your own records. The headline numbers are what is pending, what is payable now, and what has been paid.

**Payouts**: set your method under Payouts before your first payment. Depending on the business that is a bank transfer they run by hand, or an onboarding link for a payment provider, which asks you for your details directly and never shows them to the business or to us. Payments go out on the program's schedule once your payable balance is over the threshold. A failed payout tells you why, and you can fix the details and be paid on the next run.

Taxes are yours to handle. The statement is designed to be usable by an accountant.

## 8. Disputes

If a sale of yours is missing, attributed to someone else, or paid at the wrong amount, open a dispute (Disputes → New dispute) with the order reference and what you expected. The business reviews it, you both discuss it in the thread, and the outcome is one of: the sale is restored to you, it is reattributed, or the dispute is rejected with a reason. You are notified at every step.

## 9. Notifications, texts and your data

**Notifications** is your inbox for applications, sales, payouts, campaigns and dispute updates; the bell shows what is unread. Under Profile you choose which categories reach you in the app and by email. Account and security messages always go out.

**Texts**: if the business has SMS or WhatsApp connected, you can opt in under Profile with your phone number. You consent explicitly, and replying STOP opts you out at any time.

**Your data**: Profile → data requests. You can ask for your personal data to be erased. The business carries it out once nothing is owed to you; your financial records stay, because they are the business's books, but your identity is anonymised and your login disabled.

## 10. Doing well at this

- One link per placement, so you can see what actually converts.
- Send people to the page that matches the promise you made; a mismatched landing page is the most common reason good traffic does not convert.
- Check Conversions weekly rather than daily: most programs have a holding period, so today's sale is not today's money.
- If the business runs a campaign, join it before you post, not after: the better rate applies to sales made while it is live.
- Read the terms once, properly. Discount-site posting, brand bidding and coupon-scraping are the three things most programs forbid.
